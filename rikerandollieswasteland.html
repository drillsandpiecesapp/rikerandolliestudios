Your first ten minutes

  1. Type /menu (or /info). Press F1 and type bind f2 romenu.open so F2 opens and closes the menu. Esc can't close it.
  2. Read the rules.
  3. Claim your Starter kit: /kit starter, or the Kits tab. You get it once per wipe.
  4. Build a base, place a tool cupboard, and set a home with /sethome base while standing in it.
  5. Earn PawCoins from zombies, raid bases, bosses, events and quests, and spend them in the Shop.
  6. Link your Discord with /discord, and vote once a day with /vote for Treats.

Rules

  1. Be respectful. No harassment, threats, stalking or impersonation (including impersonating staff).
  2. No hate. No discriminatory language, names or signs.
  3. No doxxing. Never share anyone's personal info.
  4. No cheating. No hacks, scripts, macros or exploits. Report bugs instead.
  5. No account sharing, even with permission.
  6. No advertising or spam, including other servers.
  7. Keep it family friendly. Signs, names and chat must be age appropriate. Players must be 13+.
  8. PvE means PvE. No griefing, base blocking or loot stealing outside the PvP Monument.
  9. Report problems to staff in game or with a Discord ticket.
  10. Chat and PMs are logged. Admins can see private messages, to keep the community safe.

Staff can warn, mute, kick or ban for breaking these. Moderation is at the sole discretion of the admins and staff, and no reason is required.

Death and drops

  • Nothing you carry drops in PvE. When you die outside the PvP Monument, your inventory, hotbar and clothing are saved and put back in the same slots when you respawn, with condition, ammo, skins and attachments. Your corpse is empty.
  • This works for sleepers too, and survives a server restart while you're dead.
  • The one exception: at the PvP Monument, the item in your hands drops when you die while PvP-active. Everything else is kept.
  • Your backpack never drops.

PvP Monument (Power Plant)

  • The Power Plant is the PvP zone: 150 m around the monument. A red PvP ZONE banner shows while you're inside, and chat warns you as you enter and leave.
  • Damage counts only when both players are PvP-active. Nobody outside can shoot in, and nobody inside can shoot out at PvE players.
  • PvP timer: after you leave the zone you stay PvP-active for 60 seconds, shown on screen. Going back in resets it. Logging out leaves a sleeper that's PvP-active until the timer ends.
  • Drops: only the item in your hands drops on death.
  • Building: only sleeping bags and beds can be placed in the zone.
  • While PvP-active: teleports, /rec, backpack "Use items", /nv and /sunny are off, and Fighter Trade damage doesn't apply.
  • Worth the risk: the zone has its own zombies and its own boss, the Power Plant Overseer.

The /menu

Open it with /menu, or bind a key: F1, then bind f2 romenu.open (press it again to close). The header shows your PawCoins and Treats live, and a Theme button that cycles Green, Gold and Rust (saved for you).

Tabs, left to right: Info · Wallet · Shop · VIP · Stats · Skills · Kits · Backpack · Teleport · Clan · Quests. Many commands open a tab directly, such as /vip, /stats, /grit, /kit, /homes, /clan and /quests.

Info tab

Pages: Welcome, Rules, Wipes, Commands and Perks (both with Free, Wastelander, Tracker, Hound and Pack Leader tabs), and Links (copyable Discord, store and website links). It opens by itself on your first join, and again when the rules change.

Shortcuts: /info, /welcome, /rules, /wipe (or /wipes), /commands, /perks, /link (or /links).

Wallet and PawCoins

PawCoins are the server's money. The wallet is virtual: coins can't be dropped, stolen or lost, and they're never wiped. Check your balance with /coins (or /pawcoins, /wallet) or the Wallet tab.

How to earn them

PawCoin Pouches

The Wallet tab turns coins into PawCoin Pouches (100, 500 or 1,000, no fee): a gift box with the coin icon. Hand it to a friend, trade it, or keep it in a box. Right-click it and choose Unwrap to put the coins back in your wallet. Pouches also come from crates, bosses and events.

Shop

  • Categories on the left (Resources, Components, Medical, Ammo, Raid Bases, Titles and more), item cards on the right with x1, x5 and x10 buttons.
  • Every buy asks you to confirm, and checks your inventory has room before it charges you.
  • Some items have a daily limit, which resets at midnight UTC.
  • Raid Bases: buy a raid base of any tier (see Raid bases), with PawCoins or Treats.
  • Titles: chat titles you buy with Treats.
  • Some categories are priced in Treats instead of PawCoins.

Treats, votes and titles

Treats are the second currency, earned only by voting for the server. Every player earns the same; VIP gives no bonus. Treats are never wiped.

  • Vote once every 24 hours on the server list site, then type /vote to collect (it also checks by itself 20 seconds after you join, and reminds you every hour when you can vote again).
  • 1 Treat per vote, plus 1 bonus Treat on every 7th vote in a row. A vote within 48 hours of the last one keeps your streak.
  • /treats shows your Treats, streak and when you can vote next.

Stats and leaderboards

/stats opens the Stats tab: your stats this wipe and lifetime on the left, a top-10 leaderboard on the right with a button per stat and a This wipe / Lifetime switch. Your row is highlighted and your rank shown. Other players' stats only show on leaderboards.

Stats: zombies, NPCs, bosses, animals, PvP kills, raids cleared, deaths, resources gathered, items crafted, PawCoins earned, trades completed and active play time. In chat: /top <stat> [life], with zombies, npcs, bosses, animals, pvp, raids, deaths, gathered, crafted, coins, trades or playtime. Each finished trade with another player adds 1 to Trades for both of you. It only counts for stats.

Skills: Grit, Miles and Journeys

/grit (or /skills) opens the Skills tab. A switch at the bottom picks Road, Markers, Trades or Hall of Fame. We use our own words: Grit (experience), Miles (levels), Journeys (going round again), Scrap Points (skill points) and Trades (skill trees).

Grit

You earn Grit only from things in the world: zombies, scientists and other NPCs, wild animals, bosses, raid bases, felling trees, finishing ore nodes, wild pickups, barrels and crates (first looter only). Nothing a player placed or owns pays Grit, and neither do crafting or recycling. A "+N Grit" popup shows next to your hotbar.

  • Same spot, less Grit: farming one source in one place pays in full for the first 15 in 30 minutes, then less each time, down to a fifth.
  • AFK: after 5 idle minutes the whole idle stretch earns nothing. Move, look around or do something.

Miles

Miles run from 1 to 100 and come from your Grit. Early Miles come fast, later ones slower. A Mile never goes down. Every 10th Mile is announced to the server. /grit me gives a chat summary, and /grit forecast shows how long the road takes for casual, regular and hardcore players.

Road gates: at Miles 10, 25, 50 and 75 your Mile stops until you finish that gate's quest. Your Grit keeps counting, so the Miles catch up at once when it opens.

Fatigue

Your Grit rate depends on how much you've actively played in the last 24 hours (a rolling window, kept through relogs and restarts). Chat warns you 10 minutes before a step down. It also scales Grit from bosses and raids.

Active play, last 24 hStateGrit rate
0–4 hFresh100%
4–5 hWinded75%
5–6 hWeary50%
6 h+Exhausted25%
  • Second Wind: each hour offline gives 10 boosted minutes (up to 120). They give +50% Grit and are used only while you're Fresh.
  • Trailblazer: players behind the server's average for the Year get bonus Grit while Fresh. The bonus grows through the Year, so late joiners can still catch up. Together with Second Wind it's capped at +200%.

Journeys

At Mile 100 you can start a Journey: finish the Journey trial quest, then press the button on the Skills tab or type /grit journey confirm. Your Miles go back to 1, your Journey count goes up, and the server hears about it. Your Trades and Scrap Points stay. There's no limit.

Each Journey gives a coloured J badge in your chat tag and a title: J1 Drifter, J2 Wanderer, J3 Pathfinder, J4 Roadwarden, J5 Wayfarer, J6 Nomad, J7 Outrider, J8 Waste Walker, J9 Dust Prophet, J10 Legend of the Road, J15+ Myth of the Wastes. Journeys are lifetime: the Year Wipe keeps them. /grit top shows the leaderboard (most Journeys this Year, then Mile, then Year Grit).

The Year, Year marks and the Hall of Fame

Monthly map wipes never touch Miles, Grit, Journeys or Trade ranks 1–3. Once a year the Year Wipe resets Miles, Grit, Trades and Scrap Points, and Year N+1 begins. You keep your Journeys, badge and title, Wastelander, backpack, wallet and cosmetics.

Each past Year you played adds a Year mark (•) after your J badge in chat. The top 10 of each Year enter the Hall of Fame, and their mark for that Year turns gold. /hof (or /halloffame) opens the Hall of Fame view.

What a map wipe removes from backpacks

At each map wipe, Tier 2 and Tier 3 gear is removed: anything needing a Tier 2+ workbench in the Weapon, Ammunition, Attire, Tool and Traps categories, plus the M249. Attachments are checked on their own. Everything else stays: resources, components, Tier 1 gear, meds, deployables, electrical and building items. Wearable backpacks, parachutes, mannequins, horse shoes and survey charges are never removed. Your first login after a wipe lists what was removed.

Clan

/clan opens the Clan tab. Every clan is a Rust team, so clanmates count as teammates everywhere: base access, raids, islands, team chat, the team UI and map markers. Your [TAG] shows in chat.

  • /clan create <TAG> <name>: tag 2–5 letters or numbers (unique), name up to 24 characters. Up to 8 members.
  • /clan invite <player> (invites last 5 minutes), /clan accept [TAG], /clan deny, /clan leave.
  • /clan kick, /clan promote, /clan demote, /clan leader <player> (hand over), /clan disband <TAG>.
  • /clan info <TAG>, /clan list, and /c <message> for clan chat.
  • Roles: the leader does everything; officers invite and kick plain members. If the leader leaves, the longest-serving officer takes over (else the longest-serving member).
  • Rust's own team buttons work too: they're redirected to the clan.
  • Clans are kept through map wipes.

On the tab, without a clan you get a create box, your invites and the clan list. In a clan you see members with online dots and roles, buttons for your role, an online-player list to invite from (leaders and officers), a clan chat box and Leave.

Quests and the story

/quests (or /q) opens the Quests tab: your quests, dailies, weeklies, quests you can take, road gates, the Journey trial and the story chapters, with a detail panel and Take, Hand in, Pin and Drop buttons.

  • Dailies and weeklies: you get 3 of each, picked for you. Dailies reset at 00:00 UTC, weeklies on Monday 00:00 UTC. Finishing all three weeklies pays a bonus Scrap Point and Grit.
  • Quest types: kill zombies, scientists, animals or a boss, finish raid bases, gather, craft, loot crates and barrels, visit monuments, and deliver items.
  • Rewards: PawCoins, Grit (fixed, not affected by fatigue), Scrap Points, items and unlocks.
  • Tracker: up to 3 quests on screen, under the HUD in the top right. /tracker hides or shows it, /quests track <id> pins one, or bind a key: bind <key> roquests.tracker.
  • Quest givers: the Quartermaster stands next to the /town arrival point. Press E on a quest giver to see what they offer. Some quests must be taken and handed in there.
  • Chat: /quests daily, weekly, list, info <id>, take <id>, drop <id>, turnin <id>, chat.

Building and base tools

Remove and auto-upgrade

  • /remove turns remove mode on for 60 seconds (a red bar shows the time; each removal resets it). Hit your own or your clan's blocks and deployables with a hammer. Blocks refund their grade's cost; deployables and locks come back as items. Boxes must be empty, and nothing hit in the last 30 seconds can be removed. Not in raid zones or while PvP-active.
  • /bgrade 1-4 (1 wood, 2 stone, 3 metal, 4 HQM, 0 off): every block you place is upgraded at once, paid from your inventory.

TC panel (BASE TOOLS)

Open your tool cupboard and a BASE TOOLS panel shows in the top right:

  • Upgrade the whole base to Wood, Stone, Metal or HQM. The first click shows the block count and cost; click again within 15 seconds to confirm. It pays from the TC's resources first, then your inventory, and stops when you run out.
  • Skin button under each grade cycles through the building skins you own (never DLC you don't have). Floors: All, 1, 2, 3 or 4+.
  • Mode: Downgrade brings blocks down to Twig, Wood, Stone or Metal. Free, with no refund.
  • Repair all: repairs everything as far as your resources allow, per resource. Things hit in the last 30 seconds are skipped.
  • Show range: draws the TC's building-privilege area on the ground, only for you (or /tcrange). It turns off after 5 minutes.

Chat versions: /bup 1-4 [floor], /bdown 0-3 [floor], /brepair (stand in your base).

Free code locks

  • Every door and box you place starts with a free lock for your clan. Nobody is told the code, and it can't be picked up.
  • /lock adds a free lock to an existing door or box (or removes yours). /lock private = only you, /lock clan = you and your clan. A LOCK panel with the same choices shows when you open your box.

More base tools

  • Workbench range: anywhere in a TC area you're authorized on, you get the level of the best workbench in that building, through walls.
  • No power needed for CCTV cameras, computer stations and auto turrets you place. Turrets start off (Rust only lets you authorize and load them while off): look at one and type /turret to switch it on or off.
  • Large furnaces indoors: hold one and right-click the floor or a foundation inside your base. It needs room for the whole furnace.
  • Stacking chests (Wastelander and VIP): hold a wooden box and right-click your box of the same kind, or look at it and type /stack. Heights by tier are in Perks.
  • Floating foundations (Wastelander): look at the sea (at least 0.5 m deep) and type /float for a foundation 1 m above the water. Look at its side and /float again for the next one. Costs a twig foundation; then upgrade as usual. Not in Town, raid zones, monuments or someone else's island.
  • TC limit: 5 tool cupboards per team (a solo player is a team of one). /tcs lists your team's TCs.
  • Recycler item: place it in your base like any deployable (max 2 per player, 1 per base). Pick it up with /rec pickup or two hammer hits.

Quality of life

Sorting, sending and doors

A button row shows under the loot panel when you open a box.

  • /sort (or SORT) sorts the box you have open: stacks merged, then by category and name. Works on boxes, stashes, fridges, coffins and backpack pages.
  • /autosort on|off (or AUTO-SORT) sorts boxes and backpack pages when you close them.
  • /send (PL, or SEND TO BOXES) puts items from your inventory and backpack into boxes within 20 m that already hold that item. /send inv or /send bp for one of them. /send keep 2 keeps backpack page 2 out of it (or the PAGE button under that page).
  • /splitter on|off (PL): ore you put in a furnace or refinery spreads over the slots. The panel under it shows the smelt time and wood needed, and ADD WOOD tops up the fuel.
  • /autodoor on|off: doors you open close again after 5 seconds (/autodoor 2-60 picks the seconds; /autodoor this stops it for the door you look at).
  • /qol shows these commands and your settings.

Personal recycler

/rec (W Wastelander) opens a recycler anywhere at 2× speed. Closing it gives everything back; if your inventory is full, the rest waits in the recycler for next time. Not while PvP-active, at the PvP Monument or in raid zones. /rec help explains it.

Image signs

/sil <url> (H 5 signs, PL 10) paints a PNG or JPG from the web on the picture frame, sign or banner you look at. /sil <url> raw fits the whole image instead of cropping it, a number picks the frame on multi-frame neon signs, and /sil clear removes yours and frees the slot. Only signs you could paint yourself; 30-second cooldown. Every image is logged for staff, and the rules apply (family friendly).

HUD and tracker

  • The HUD in the top right shows your map grid and monument, the weather and temperature, and event chips: Cargo, Lg Rig, Sm Rig (green while that rig's locked crate is waiting) and Heli. /hud hides or shows it.
  • The quest tracker sits under it. /tracker hides or shows it.

Night vision and clear weather

  • /nv gives you daylight; /nv <hour> picks the time (for example /nv 10). The server's night still happens, and zombies still get their night boost.
  • /sunny clears the weather just for you: no rain, fog, clouds or storms.
  • Both switch off at the PvP Monument and during the PvP timer, and come back after.

Chat, PMs and Discord

Chat names look like [W|PL] [Good Pup] [TAG] Name: hi: your tier codes (plus staff titles, your J badge and Year marks), your Treat title, a [Booster] tag for Discord boosters, and your clan tag.

  • /pm <name> <message> sends a private message; /r <message> replies to your last one.
  • /ignore <name> ignores or unignores someone (their PMs and chat are hidden from you); /ignore list. Staff can't be ignored.
  • /c <message> is clan chat.
  • Chat and PMs are logged, and staff can read PMs in a private Discord channel, to keep the community safe.

Linking your Discord

  1. Join the Discord.
  2. Type /discord in game (or open /menu > Info > Links > Discord and press Get my link code). You get a 6-letter code (valid 10 minutes).
  3. Post just the code in #how-to-link. The bot links your accounts and deletes the message.

Linking gives you the Linked role, syncs your Wastelander and VIP roles, and gives Server Boosters a [Booster] tag in game. /discord unlink undoes it. Global chat is shared both ways with the Discord's in-game chat channel.

Trading with players

Trade items, PawCoins and Treats with another player safely, anywhere on the map. Same for everyone.

  1. /trade <name> sends a request. They have 30 seconds to type /trade accept (or /trade decline).
  2. You both get a trade window: your own offer box (the loot panel, 12 slots) and, above it, what the other player offers, live. Type PawCoins and Treats into the fields. List in chat prints their offer with condition and skins.
  3. When the offer looks right, press Accept. Once you both have, a 3 second countdown runs. Any change to either offer clears both accepts and stops the countdown, so nobody can swap something at the last second.
  4. The swap happens all at once. If one of you doesn't have a free inventory slot for every stack coming in, nothing moves.
  • Closing the window, /trade cancel, leaving, dying or a server restart gives back everything you offered. Coins stay in your wallet until the swap.
  • No trading at the PvP Monument, while PvP-active, right after taking damage, when downed, in a seat or vehicle, or in a raid zone.
  • /trade block turns trade requests off (and on again). Players you /ignore can't send you requests.
  • Every trade is logged for staff.

Vehicle garage

Buy a vehicle once with PawCoins and it's yours to keep: bring it out when you need it, put it away when you're done. Same prices for everyone.

  • /buy (or /garage) opens the GARAGE tab in /menu. /buy minicopter buys in chat. Vehicles: minicopter, scrap heli, attack heli, rowboat, RHIB, tugboat, horse (with a saddle), cars with 2, 3 or 4 modules, motorbike, motorbike with sidecar, bicycle and snowmobile. Your coins are only taken once the vehicle is out, and if it doesn't make it, you get them back.
  • /spawn <name> brings it out near you: boats on water next to you, helis on flat open ground, never inside a building or on someone else's base.
  • /recall (or /store) puts it back in your garage with its fuel, cargo, car modules and engine parts, ready for next time. /recall all puts everything away. Get everyone off first.
  • Only you and your clan can drive it, open its fuel or storage, or push it. /garage allow <name> lets a friend use your vehicles too (/garage unallow <name>). Other players can't damage it, except at the PvP Monument when you're both PvP-active.
  • Destroyed? /garage repair <name> (or the Repair button) gets it back for half the price. Whatever was inside drops where it was destroyed.
  • A vehicle left alone for 30 minutes with nobody near goes back to your garage by itself, with everything still in it.
  • No spawning or recalling while PvP-active, at the PvP Monument or in a raid zone. No spawning in Town.
  • Your vehicles are kept through map wipes. The fuel and cargo in them are cleared at the wipe.
  • Wastelander: your garage minicopter never decays.

Town and travel

  • Town is the market: a safe zone on its own island, under a blue dome, with server shops. Nothing can damage players or things inside, and only staff build there. It's marked on the map ("Town: safe zone").
  • /town (or /market) teleports you there: 10-second countdown, 15-minute cooldown, free.
  • /outpost and /bandit teleport you to Outpost and Bandit Camp, with the same countdown and cooldown. You arrive at a random safe spot.
  • The same teleport blocks apply.
  • The Quartermaster quest giver waits next to the Town arrival point.

Coming soon: rentable player stalls and PawCoin shops at the market.

Raid bases

NPC-guarded bases spawn on flat land around the map, away from Town, islands, monuments and player bases. Each has a grey dome, a coloured map circle with a label, and a banner under the compass showing tier, owner, loot left, guards left and the countdown.

Nightmare5x5 armored with a core room, 4 auto turrets1,000 PawCoins or 25 Treats500
  • Free bases (Easy and Medium) spawn on their own, more with more players online. The first player to hit or loot one claims it.
  • Bought raids: /buyraid <tier> (add treats to pay in Treats), or the Shop's Raid Bases category. One bought raid per player at a time; refunded if it can't spawn.
  • The owner, their clan and anyone they /inviteraid <player> can damage the base and guards, loot and build there. Everyone else is blocked.
  • Inside: code-locked doors to break, scientists on guard (some posted at windows and on the roof), zombies, loot boxes and a TC full of resources that raiders are authorized on, so you can place ladders and barricades.
  • Timers: 30 minutes to start; once started, no limit while a raider stays within 100 m, but it's abandoned after 10 minutes with nobody near. It despawns 2 minutes after the last box is emptied.
  • Payout is split evenly among the raiders nearby when the loot is cleared.
  • /raids lists the raid bases on the map.

Abandoned bases

  • If nobody who keeps a base alive (the TC owner, their clan, and anyone authorized on the TC) logs in for 5 days, the base becomes a PvE raidable base. Warnings go out on day 3 and day 4 (in Discord, and by DM if you linked your account).
  • It gets a purple map marker. /abandoned lists them with their claim status.
  • The first raider claims it (damage, loot or authorize on its TC) for themselves, their clan and anyone they /inviteraid. The claim drops after 10 minutes with no claimer within 100 m.
  • The old locks stay locked, so raiders break in like a real raid. Everything inside is loot (backpacks are safe anyway).
  • If an owner logs back in, the base is protected again.

Private islands

A few private islands (Riker's Rock, Ollie's Isle, Paw Print Cay and Pack Leader Point) are build spots for the whole wipe. Each has a bubble around it and a map marker: green = free, purple = claimed.

  • Get an Island Deed from the store (coming soon), then /island claim [number] on a free island. If all are taken, the deed waits for the next wipe.
  • One per clan. Islands are freed at every map wipe; an unused deed carries over.
  • The owner's clan is allowed automatically. /island invite <name> and /island kick <name> add or remove others; /island admin <name> lets a clanmate invite and kick (/island unadmin).
  • Unlocked: anyone can visit, but only allowed players can build, loot, use doors and boxes, or deal damage. Locked (/island lock, a toggle): outsiders are kept out of the bubble, boats and minicopters included.
  • /island shows your status, /island list every island.

Oil rigs

  • The player who starts the locked-crate hack at the Large Oil Rig or the Oil Rig gets that rig for their team while the crate hacks and for 5 minutes after it opens. A purple dome shows while it's locked.
  • Anyone else is pushed out past the edge (boats and helis too) and told whose rig it is.
  • The HUD's rig chips are green while a fresh crate is waiting, and go grey once someone starts hacking it. Rig, Cargo Ship and Patrol Heli events are also posted in the Discord.

The Fish Pistol

A boss-only special weapon that fires a fish like a rocket, exploding on impact. HV Fish (small and medium fish) fly fast and flat; Rocket Fish (large fish) arc and hit hard. It has two 10-fish magazines: /ammo switches between them and R reloads the active one. One shot every 3 seconds. It damages buildings, but other players' bases stay protected by the PvE rules.
