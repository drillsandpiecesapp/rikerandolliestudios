Recipes, with credit to the cook

Your own recipe box
  • Save a recipe from a website by link, by sharing a page to the app, or from the app’s built-in browser
  • Every saved recipe keeps the link and credit to the site it came from
  • Sort recipes into your own categories and types, such as Chicken or Gluten free
  • Write your own recipes, or read one from a photo
  • Websites whose owners asked not to be saved are respected

Grocery list

Always with you at the store
  • Add items by hand or by scanning a barcode
  • Add amounts, and check things off as you shop

Kitchen inventory

Pantry, fridge, freezer and spices
  • Keep a list for each place you store food
  • Add amounts, “running low” marks and use-by dates

Barcodes and receipts

Less typing
  • Scan a barcode to add a product, and the app remembers it for next time
  • Read items from a photo of a receipt, checking them before you save

Make it yours

Look and feel
  • Theme, accent color, text size and compact view
  • Choose your own background photo
  • Export a backup, or turn on automatic backup, to a place you choose

About ads and your privacy

The app is free and shows a small banner ad from Google AdMob. Google’s ad service may collect information such as your device’s advertising ID to show ads, and where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent first. You can pay once to remove ads. Your recipes, grocery list and kitchen lists stay on your phone, and I never receive them.
