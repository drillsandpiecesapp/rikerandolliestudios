How I build apps, and custom app work

Every app from Riker & Ollie Studios starts with a real need and one person who cares how it feels to use. AI tools help me build faster, so more of my time goes to design, testing and listening.

Building with AI, with a person in charge

I use AI tools as a creative and technical partner. They help me write and review code, explore ideas, draft text and create some of the artwork you see on this site and in my apps. Think of it as having a tireless pair of extra hands at the workbench.

What AI helps with

  • Writing and reviewing code, so features arrive sooner
  • Trying out layouts and ideas before settling on one
  • Creating some images and illustrations, including the cartoon portraits of Riker and Ollie
  • Checking details such as accessibility, spelling and clear wording

What stays human

  • Deciding what each app should do, and what it should leave out
  • Designing the experience and keeping it simple
  • Testing on real phones and fixing what feels wrong
  • Privacy choices, and every promise on these pages

The result is small, focused apps built by one developer in Oregon, with the speed and polish that used to take a larger team. You can read how each app handles your information in its privacy policy.

Let’s build your app

I’m happy to talk about developing an Android app to your specifications, on contract. It might be a tool for your hobby, a companion for a game, or a small business idea you want to test. Tell me about it using the form below. It takes about ten minutes, and the more you share, the better I can respond with questions, a rough timeline and a quote.

How the form works: when you press the button, your own email app opens with your answers filled in, addressed to melissa@rikerandolliestudios.com. Nothing is sent until you press Send in your email app, and this website does not collect or store your answers. If your email app doesn’t open, you can copy the text and email it yourself.
