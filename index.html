About the studio

Riker & Ollie Studios LLC is a small, independent Android app studio based in Oregon. I design, build and test every app myself, so there is no big team between you and the person making the updates.

My apps follow a minimalist design philosophy with a strong focus on ease of use. Every feature is chosen to be intuitive, uncluttered and genuinely useful, so you can spend less time learning software and more time enjoying your hobbies and favorite games.
